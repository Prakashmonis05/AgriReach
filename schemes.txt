SCHEME 1: PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN)
------------------------------------------------------
Launch Year: December 2018
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
Scheme Type: Income Support
Status: Active (20th installment released August 2025)

OBJECTIVE:
Direct income support to all landholding farmers to supplement financial needs for procuring agricultural inputs and ensuring proper crop health

FINANCIAL BENEFIT:
- ₹6,000 per year per farmer family
- Paid in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000 each (every 4 months)
- Direct Benefit Transfer to Aadhaar-linked bank accounts

ELIGIBILITY:
- All landholding farmers (initially for small & marginal farmers only)
- Extended to all farmers regardless of land size from June 2019
- State/UT governments identify eligible farmer families

BENEFICIARIES: Over 9 crore farmers
TOTAL DISBURSEMENT: Over ₹3.45 lakh crore (as of 18th installment)

KEY FEATURES:
- Central Sector Scheme (100% funded by Government of India)
- Aadhaar linking and e-KYC mandatory
- No restrictions on utilization of funds
- Protects farmers from moneylenders

EXCLUSIONS:
- Government employees and their families
- Income taxpayers
- Professional practitioners (doctors, lawyers, CAs, engineers, architects)
- Present/former MPs, MLAs, Ministers, Constitutional post holders
- Retired pensioners with pension ≥ ₹10,000/month

APPLICATION MODE: Online via PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in)
HELPLINE: PM-KISAN helpline


SCHEME 2: PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY)
-------------------------------------------------
Launch Year: April 2016
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
Scheme Type: Crop Insurance
Status: Active

OBJECTIVE:
Provide comprehensive insurance coverage against crop losses due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases

FINANCIAL BENEFIT:
Premium rates (paid by farmers):
- Kharif crops: 2% of sum insured
- Rabi crops: 1.5% of sum insured
- Horticultural/Commercial crops: 5% of sum insured
- Balance premium subsidized by Central and State governments

SPECIAL PROVISIONS:
- In Assam: Farmer share of premium up to 1 hectare is fully subsidized (₹1 only)
- Covers pre-sowing to post-harvest risks
- Add-on coverage for localized calamities

ELIGIBILITY:
All farmers (loanee and non-loanee)

COVERAGE UNIT: Block/Gram Panchayat level

KEY FEATURES:
- Technology-enabled claims settlement
- Use of smartphones, drones, remote sensing for crop loss assessment
- Weather-based triggers for faster payouts
- Coverage for prevented sowing, post-harvest losses

APPLICATION MODE: Online via PMFBY portal, Common Service Centers, banks
WEBSITE: pmfby.gov.in


SCHEME 3: PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN MAANDHAN YOJANA (PM-KMY)
------------------------------------------------------
Launch Year: September 2019
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
Scheme Type: Old Age Pension
Status: Active

OBJECTIVE:
Provide old age social security and pension support to small and marginal farmers

FINANCIAL BENEFIT:
- ₹3,000 monthly pension after attaining 60 years of age
- Contributory pension scheme

ELIGIBILITY:

KEY FEATURES:

- Voluntary enrollment and exit

APPLICATION MODE: Through Common Service Centers and online portal
WEBSITE: maandhan.in


SCHEME 4: KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) SCHEME
---------------------------------------
Launch Year: 1998 (Revised and strengthened)
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
Scheme Type: Credit Support
Status: Active

OBJECTIVE:
Provide adequate and timely credit support to farmers for agricultural operations

FINANCIAL BENEFIT:
- Short-term crop loans at 7% interest rate
- Interest subvention of 2% for prompt repayment (effective rate: 4%)
- Additional 3% interest subvention for timely repayment
- Credit limit based on cropping pattern and scale of finance

COVERAGE:
- Crop cultivation (agricultural activities)
- Post-harvest expenses
- Produce marketing loan
- Consumption needs of farmer household
- Working capital for maintenance of farm assets
- Investment credit for agriculture and allied activities
- Animal husbandry, fisheries, dairy

ELIGIBILITY:
All farmers (individual/joint borrowers, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers)

KEY FEATURES:
- Single window facility
- Revolving cash credit
- Simplified documentation
- Flexibility in withdrawals and repayments
- Insurance coverage (Crop and Personal Accident)

APPLICATION MODE: Through all scheduled commercial banks, RRBs, cooperative banks
TARGET: Saturation coverage of all eligible farmers


SCHEME 5: SOIL HEALTH CARD SCHEME
--------------------------------
Launch Year: February 19, 2015
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
Scheme Type: Soil Management
Status: Active
Duration: 3 years (2015-2018), now continuing
Outlay: ₹568 crore

OBJECTIVE:
Assess soil health and provide crop-wise recommendations to improve soil fertility and productivity

MOTTO: "Swasth Dhara, Khet Haraa" (Healthy Soil, Green Fields)

FINANCIAL BENEFIT:
- Free soil testing
- Printed Soil Health Card for each farmer
- Crop-wise nutrient recommendations

SOIL PARAMETERS TESTED (12):
1. Nitrogen (N)
2. Phosphorus (P)
3. Potassium (K)
4. Sulphur (S)
5. INTEREST-FREE CROP LOAN:
   - Up to ₹50,000 per family
   - 0% interest for timely repayment
   - State pays interest subvention

BENEFICIARIES: Over 50 lakh farming families

KEY FEATURES:
- Comprehensive support system
- Covers landless agricultural workers
- Social security net
- Quick disbursal through DBT
- Financial inclusion

ELIGIBILITY:
Small, marginal, and landless agricultural households in Odisha

STATE: HARYANA
=============

SCHEME 1: BHAVANTAR BHARPAI YOJANA (BBY)
---------------------------------------
Launch Year: 2018
Department: Agriculture & Farmers Welfare Department, Haryana
Type: Price Deficiency Payment
Status: Active (Seasonal implementation)

OBJECTIVE:
Protect farmers from distress sale of horticulture crops

COVERED CROPS:
Tomato, potato, onion, cauliflower, cabbage, carrot, peas, brinjal, okra, bottle gourd, and others (total 21 vegetables)

FINANCIAL BENEFIT:
- Direct payment of difference between MSP/Fair Remunerative Price (FRP) and actual market price
- Compensation paid if market price falls below MSP
- Based on modal price in mandis

ELIGIBILITY:
All farmers selling in notified mandis in Haryana

KEY FEATURES:
- Protects vegetable growers from price crash
- No physical procurement by government
- Farmer sells in open market
- Electronic registration in mandis
- Quick payment through DBT

LIMITATIONS:
- Registration required at time of sale
- Maximum limit per farmer per crop
- Only for notified mandis
